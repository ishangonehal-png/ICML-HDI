# Cohort & evidence-bundle integrity audit

Input-side audit of the released HDI artifacts: does the cohort split and the
`evidence_bundles/` content match the cohort and four-condition definitions the
paper states? Generated by `scripts/eval/verify_cohort_bundles.py` (stdlib, offline).

## What reproduces (cohort)

- **C1 N=119**: PASS — every run has 119 cases.
- **C2 tertile counts 40/40/39**: PASS — Hot=40,Warm=40,Cold=39, matching paper Appendix l.639 exactly.
- **C3 tertile partition**: PASS — `io_tier` is a strict, non-overlapping tertile cut of `io_score` (Cold<Warm<Hot).
- **C4 cohort shared across models**: PASS — all four run directories carry the identical (case_id, io_tier) set.
- **B1 baseline evidence-free**: PASS — every `baseline` bundle has an empty pathology block, so the ungrounded (HDI denominator) leg is legitimate.

## What does NOT reproduce (multimodal content)

- **C5/B3/B4 RNA present**: FAIL — `full_multimodal` bundles with any RNA value: 0/119. Every bundle has `rna_available:false`, `transcriptomics` all null, fusion transcriptomics contribution 0.0%. The paper (l.639) claims the cohort has "complete pathology and RNA layers"; methods (l.109) define `full_multimodal` as pathology+RNA+clinical+tier.
- **B4 clinical present**: FAIL — `full_multimodal` bundles with a clinical block: 0/119. `case_selection.csv` stamps `has_clinical=True` (hardcoded in `run_bionlp_eval.py` as "deterministic fallback"), but no clinical layer is present in the bundle. (Clinical *proxy* text does leak via `fusion.modality_contributions.clinical.summary`.)
- **B depth-of-grounding gradient present**: FAIL — cases where the three grounded bundles are byte-identical: 119/119. `pathology_only`, `pathology_rna`, and `full_multimodal` present the *same* pathology-only bundle to the model. There is no evidence-content gradient to ablate; the per-config differences in Table 6 (`tab:gradient`, reduced in the tab:gradient PR) come from generation stochasticity and the config label announced in the prompt, not from evidence depth. Summaries still differ (0/119 identical `pathology_only` vs `pathology_rna`).
