# Semantic-overlap probe — offline reproduction status

_Backend: chartfidf (offline lexical proxy). Generated by `scripts/eval/compute_semantic_overlap.py`._

## What this is

The semantic-overlap probe (paper §"Semantic claim overlap", Tables `tab:panel` / `tab:cis` / `tab:tau-sens`) is the only paper pillar with **no committed reduction**: it requires per-claim `text-embedding-3-small` vectors that are not in the repo. This script commits the reduction and runs it with a deterministic **offline lexical proxy** (char 3/4/5-gram TF-IDF cosine). The proxy is a *different measurement* from the paper's dense embedding: absolute values and tau-thresholds are not comparable, only the cross-model **ordering** and the "is overlap low?" question transfer.

## What reproduces and what does not

- **Overlap is low across all models** — the paper's headline directional claim. Under the lexical proxy no grounded claim is a near-duplicate of a same-patient baseline claim (overlap at the paper's dense tau's is 0.0000 for every model; even at a permissive lexical tau=0.40 it stays <0.02). Grounded claims are **not verbatim recyclings** of baseline text for any model. *Corroborated (directionally).*

- **gpt-4o-mini has the highest overlap** (paper: 0.115 @0.80, the max). Proxy ranks `gpt-4o-mini` highest by mean max-sim → holds under mean max-sim (but statistic-fragile, see below).

- **overlap does not track HDI: gpt-5.4-mini's overlap < gpt-4o-mini's** (the paper's specific structural claim, 0.032 < 0.115). Proxy mean max-sim 0.1302 vs 0.1374 → holds under mean max-sim (but statistic-fragile, see below).

- **The proxy corroboration above is statistic-fragile.** Ranked by overlap@0.30 instead of mean max-sim the order becomes gpt-5.4-mini > gemini-3-flash-preview > gemini-2.5-flash > gpt-4o-mini — here gpt-4o-mini is *lowest*, contradicting the two bullets above. A lexical proxy simply does not have a stable read on the paper's ordering; treat the offline number as a low-overlap floor, not a reproduction of the paper's ranking.

- **The fine cross-model ordering is NOT reproducible offline.** Paper overlap@0.80 order (high→low): gpt-4o-mini > gemini-3-flash-preview > gpt-5.4-mini > gemini-2.5-flash. Proxy mean-max-sim order: gpt-4o-mini > gpt-5.4-mini > gemini-2.5-flash > gemini-3-flash-preview. The two agree at the top (gpt-4o-mini) but diverge in the middle: gemini-3-flash-preview is 2nd in the paper but last under the proxy. The paper's overlap ordering — and hence the **Mode B** half of the taxonomy (gpt-5.4-mini: HDI≤0.4 AND overlap@0.80≤0.05) — lives in the dense paraphrase space and **cannot be verified from committed artifacts** without the `text-embedding-3-small` vectors.
