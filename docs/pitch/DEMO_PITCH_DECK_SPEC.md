# Demo Pitch Deck Spec (5 Slides)

This is a structured implementation spec for Canva/PPTX recreation.

## Audience + Constraints
- Audience: hackathon judges (mixed technical + business)
- Tone: pragmatic, clinically grounded
- Length: 5 slides, ultra-compact
- Guardrails:
  - Decision support, not autonomous treatment recommendation
  - No universal all-cancer claim
  - No production clinical validation claim

## Narrative Thread
1. Problem: fragmented oncology workflow in community settings
2. Niche: early-line lung, breast, colon
3. Product now: visualization + IO readiness + trial cues + similarity + grounded agent
4. Credibility: clinician-in-the-loop framing
5. Expansion: treatment sequencing, external data, EHR integration

## Slide-by-Slide Build Spec

### Slide 1
**Objective**: establish positioning quickly.

**Title**: Genomics-First Community Oncology Copilot

**Body (max 4 bullets)**
- Early-line decision support for lung, breast, and colon cancer
- One-screen patient synthesis: clinical timeline + molecular context
- Immunotherapy-readiness + trial prioritization + similar-case context
- Built for community oncology workflow speed

**Layout**
- Left: title + bullets
- Right: product screenshot or dashboard mock

**Visual style**
- Dark navy background, cyan highlights, clean sans-serif

---

### Slide 2
**Objective**: define the clinical inefficiency.

**Title**: Why This Matters in Real Workflow

**Body (max 4 bullets)**
- Oncology data is fragmented across multiple screens and specialties
- Time-sensitive treatment decisions happen with incomplete synthesis
- Community settings often lack immediate subspecialist access
- Physician feedback: genomics + timeline are highest-value at point of care

**Layout**
- Before/after split visualization
- Keep text minimal and large

**Evidence framing**
- Cite physician consultation insight verbally during narration

---

### Slide 3
**Objective**: show concrete product capability now.

**Title**: What We Have Built (Now)

**Body (max 5 bullets)**
- One-screen multimodal workspace (clinical + pathology + transcriptomics)
- IO readiness signal with transparent component breakdown
- Trial prioritization cues with patient-context rationale
- Similar-patient retrieval over internal cohort features
- Agent Q&A grounded in patient context and literature

**Layout**
- 5 feature cards or one annotated screenshot

**Presenter note**
- Keep this to “capability demo,” not outcome claims

---

### Slide 4
**Objective**: justify scoped clinical niche.

**Title**: Why Lung, Breast, and Colon First

**Body (max 4 bullets)**
- These cancers frequently use biomarker/genomic context in treatment planning
- Strong fit for immunotherapy-response support and trial triage
- Matches physician guidance on where molecular context most changes care
- Deliberate scope: focused impact over broad but shallow coverage

**Layout**
- 3-column focus panel (Lung / Breast / Colon)

**Guardrail callout**
- “Focused demo scope; not universal all-cancer recommendation engine.”

---

### Slide 5
**Objective**: show expansion and ask.

**Title**: From Demo to Clinical Workflow Integration

**Body (max 5 bullets)**
- Expand from IO signal to full treatment sequencing support (IO/chemo/radiation context)
- Extend similar-case retrieval to external networks/registries + specialist routing
- Upgrade trial prioritization to site-aware operational ranking
- Integrate into EHR-native workflow with clinician-in-the-loop governance
- CTA: pilot with community oncology groups for workflow validation

**Layout**
- Horizontal roadmap: Now → Next → Integration → Pilot

**Closing line (spoken)**
- “From fragmented oncology charts to one decision-ready screen.”

## Timing Spec
- Full mode: ~30 seconds/slide (total ~2.5 minutes)
- Fast mode: ~15 seconds/slide (total ~75 seconds)

## QA Checklist Before Recording
1. Exactly 5 slides
2. No bullets over 2 lines on screen
3. No autonomous-treatment wording
4. Scope explicitly limited to lung/breast/colon
5. Final slide includes pilot CTA
