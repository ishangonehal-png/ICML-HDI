## Slide 2 — Clinical Inefficiency / Why Now
**Title**  
Why This Matters in Real Workflow

**Bullets (on-screen, keep as-is)**
- Oncology data is fragmented across multiple screens and specialties
- Time-sensitive treatment decisions happen with incomplete synthesis
- Community settings often lack immediate subspecialist access
- Physician feedback: genomics + timeline are highest-value at point of care

**Speaker Notes (30s)**
From our physician consultation, the biggest pain is not one missing model, it’s fragmented workflow: biopsy, surgery, chemo, radiation, and molecular data live in different places. Clinicians need one high-confidence summary quickly, especially in community settings.

**Speaker Notes (15s)**
The core problem is workflow fragmentation. Clinicians need a fast, unified timeline plus genomics context, not another disconnected tool.

**Visual Prompt (Canva)**
Before/after layout: left side “fragmented screens,” right side “single consolidated oncology view.”

**Do Not Overclaim**
Do not claim elimination of care delays; position as reducing synthesis burden.
